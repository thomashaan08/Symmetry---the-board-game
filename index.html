SYMMETRY

Abstract Territory Board Game

Move Log

White starts. Players alternate placing stones on empty cells.

After a placement, every fully-filled square (2×2 and larger) that is horizontally or vertically mirror-symmetric flips all its stones to the placing player's color.

Diagonal symmetry does not count. Multiple squares may flip per move.

The board fills up. Most stones of your color wins.
